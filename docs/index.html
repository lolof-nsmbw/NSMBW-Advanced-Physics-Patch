NSMBW Advanced Physics Patch — Beta Feature Overview

Beta Feature Overview

A physics and gameplay overhaul for New Super Mario Bros. Wii that brings Super Mario World, Super Mario Maker, and New Super Mario Bros. U mechanics — plus original custom features — into the vanilla engine, with full NewerSMBW compatibility.

Beta testing

We are recruiting playtesters

The patch is feature-complete for this beta pass. What it needs now is people who will break it on purpose. Everyone joins as a Beta Tester — test whichever way fits how you play.

01

Solo & Co-op Playtesters

Real courses, custom levels, local co-op. Tell us where the new physics feels off or causes a softlock.

02

Kaizo & Tech Players

Stress-test the tight frame windows: up-throw re-grabs, wall-kick spin jumps, shell juggles, and spring setups.

03

Level Creators

Build levels around the new mechanics in Reggie! Tell us what creators need: clearer visual cues or more reliable setups.
